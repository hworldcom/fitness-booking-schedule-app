## Objective and context

Give athletes an accessible My coaches list and mutual connection controls after a qualifying confirmed session. Implement against the [current specification](../../../docs/mvp-spec.md#49-connect-after-a-confirmed-session); this ticket records the in-progress implementation and its validation; completion remains subject to the outstanding checks below.

## Scope and non-goals

- In scope: eligible session Connect/request state, incoming/outgoing connection requests, accepted My coaches list, current public coach identity/portrait, complete paginated shared request/booking-history navigation, safe public-profile links, disconnection/reconnection and failure recovery.
- Out of scope: coach My athletes, backend authority, public athlete profiles, instant rebooking, chat, notifications and native runtime.

## Expected behavior and edge cases

- Either participant can disconnect without deleting/resetting shared history. Fresh mutually accepted reconnection restores a complete paginated history view for the same pair, including unsuccessful booking requests and cancelled sessions. Keep original identifiers/statuses and show the selected request/booking conversation once.
- Shared conversation/plan/note details remain owned by COR0015. History links reopen original contexts when those interfaces are delivered; terminal/cancelled content stays read-only and coach-private content never enters shared recovery. Existing exact-participant access survives disconnection.

- The athlete may send or withdraw its request and accept/decline a coach's incoming request according to server state. Coaches training as athletes use the same account and flow.
- My coaches separates accepted connections from incoming/outgoing requests and booking history. A coach is shown once per relationship regardless of session count.
- Another-session actions follow the existing public profile and approval-request flow. Hidden/suspended/unavailable coaches keep truthful retained relationship identity without exposing a rebooking action that cannot succeed.
- Removing a connection preserves bookings and their participant access; revoked connection-only data disappears after the authoritative result.
- Stale qualifying sessions, conflicts, unavailable services and missing portraits keep usable feedback and initials.

### Changes and rationale

My sessions now includes My coaches and connection options on each retained session. An athlete can send/withdraw its request, explicitly accept/decline a coach's request, view accepted coaches, disconnect and send a fresh reconnection request after a qualifying shared session. Incoming/outgoing and terminal attempts remain separate from accepted relationships and session history.

Both coach and athlete routes use one perspective-aware connection controller, list, booking controls, history panel, confirmation and media fallback. Role-specific recipient checks and original session anchors prevent the athlete view from inheriting coach labels or permissions. Existing coach behavior is retained rather than duplicating its action/recovery state machine. Source snapshots bind actor scope, perspective and each authoritative read. A server refresh clears previously derived pages even when the initial page looks identical; loading more rebuilds the displayed range from fresh authorised pages so revoked rows and stale statuses cannot return from cache.

My coaches chooses only the counterpart coach's retained name and currently public portrait. An accepted currently public coach offers the existing profile/request flow; a hidden or suspended coach retains readable relationship/history and safe controls without that shortcut. Athlete views never choose connection-derived athlete-avatar bytes. A suspended coach training as an athlete follows that separate pair's athlete permissions.

All original shared booking/request history is available through older pages. The selected request and resulting session appear once, with original IDs/statuses, and View session returns to the original athlete booking card. Either participant can remove the connection without deleting history; fresh mutual acceptance restores the same retained references. Conversation bodies/shared plans/shared notes remain independently owned by COR0015.
