## Expected behavior and edge cases

Publication rejects overlap with nonwithdrawn private occurrences/bookings or another active group session. The coach explicitly closes conflicting private occurrences first; publication never deletes bookings or closes private requests silently. Active group sessions block future overlapping private materialization/mutations. Missed-deadline expiry or cancellation releases only the group reservation; previously withdrawn private occurrences stay withdrawn. Adjacent half-open intervals do not overlap, and primary-location changes never move session snapshots.

For example, an open private occurrence on Wednesday 18:00–19:00 prevents publication of a group at that time, even at another venue. The coach closes that occurrence through the existing Schedule/Bookings tools, which retain their pending-request closure behavior. Group publication can then succeed with zero registrations and immediately reserve coach time; the minimum matters for later manual confirmation. Cancelling that group does not reopen the closed private occurrence or its requests.

An active weekly rule is a template, not an already published occurrence. Where no private occurrence exists yet, a group may publish and later recurrence generation skips its occupied interval. After release, previously uncreated eligible private occurrences may be generated; existing withdrawn occurrences remain withdrawn. Include groups beyond the private seven-day generation window, so a rolling horizon cannot later introduce an overlap. Hidden/suspended coaches' confirmed group reservations remain effective until cancellation or elapsed time; discovery visibility is not reservation authority.

A confirmed group already in progress still occupies its interval until its end, so filtering reservations only by future start would be incorrect. An undecided open session whose decision deadline passed no longer holds time once its terminal expiry is reconciled. Check both cases in private-write tests.

### Changes and rationale

Previously the application could retain group drafts and registrations but could not publish a group or protect its coach time against private scheduling. The new owner publication command checks the reviewed revision, live approval/visibility, discipline, active owned venue, Berlin times, deadline and all cross-venue group/private conflicts. It freezes the reviewed terms and records the publishing revision. An exact retry returns the same publication; stale revisions and changed venue terms require review, and terminal sessions cannot be republished. Conflicts contain no private booking or participant data.

All eight private/profile/rule write tables and group lifecycle mutations now acquire the existing dataset/coach advisory-lock key. Late row-trigger entry uses a nonblocking lock: contention aborts the transaction with one exact SQLSTATE 40001 message rather than waiting with an inverted row-lock order. The existing actor transaction wrapper retries only this known aborted outcome and the existing message-pair conflict, at most four attempts; arbitrary 40001 errors and unknown outcomes are not retried. Publication locks eligibility before the draft and rechecks database time after waiting. Mail source/card transactions retain their existing pair locks and atomicity.

Open/Confirmed groups reserve their complete half-open interval, including in-progress Confirmed sessions and hidden/suspended coaches. Private writes reconcile overlapping missed-decision groups before checking reservations. Recurrence skips occupied time without deleting weekly rules. Cancellation/expiry permits previously uncreated eligible private occurrences, while withdrawn exact coach/time intervals remain closed even after rule replacement. The existing owner closure now enters and restores the protected recurring-rule context so it can close a recurring occurrence without weakening guards or reviving requests.
