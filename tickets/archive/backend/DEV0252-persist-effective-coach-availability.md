## Objective and context

Replace the seven-day recurring-only publication boundary with authoritative 30-local-date effective availability: weekly hours plus whole-date overrides, retained reservations and explicit policy-restoration identities. ## Expected behavior and edge cases

Preserve historical seven-day occurrences and existing bookings; cancellation under a day off must not reopen capacity. Distinguish policy closures from manual closures, withdraw/reassign open hours with atomic request-card closure, and create fresh successor identities only for explicit eligible policy restoration. Future overrides may precede their entry into the horizon. Reconcile public reads/request mutations without a coach visit. Venue deactivation, suspension, groups beyond the horizon, today/29th date/DST edges and late lock waits cannot bypass eligibility.

### Changes and rationale

Delivered the effective-availability backend on 16 October 2026. Complete weekly saves and whole-date replacement/removal now carry an expected policy revision and stable operation ID. One atomic save validates all venues/dates, retains booked private and published group reservations, closes displaced pending requests with their original M8 cards, and returns retained-session warnings and closure counts. The preview executes this exact path in a rollback-only database subtransaction; it persists no policy, receipt, occurrence or card.

Generation and request enforcement cover today plus 29 Berlin dates using database time. Public reads reconcile without a coach visit and select one current identity per original interval, preferring active capacity over terminal predecessors. Original request/detail IDs and venue snapshots remain unchanged. Explicit policy changes permit fresh eligible successor identities; passive reads and historical individual-slot functions cannot revive manual closures. Private cancellation retains its original booking and reopens only current eligible venue capacity; a day off or changed venue cannot reopen obsolete hours. Venue deactivation closes custom-date hours and pending cards atomically as well as weekly hours.

The existing M7 coach lock and M8 pair/source/card boundary remain authoritative. Early reconciliation/save entry points queue on the existing coach lock; late write guards remain nonblocking to avoid inverted row/pair lock order. A protected policy-row transaction marker suppresses per-rule bumps inside one complete save; caller-set configuration flags cannot suppress revision tracking.

### Contracts, configuration, and operations

Apply additive migration `20261016000200` after the complete existing migration chain, including DEV0237/DEV0238. Its three new tables are `coach_availability_policies`, `coach_availability_date_overrides` and `coach_availability_operations`. All force row security and deny raw runtime/browser access. Existing withdrawn rows receive conservative manual provenance without changing their audit timestamps; existing bookings, original occurrences/requests/cards and location snapshots remain. New columns are `policy_revision`, `closure_reason` and `closed_policy_revision`; the rule/date unique index now excludes withdrawn predecessors while the coach-time exclusion constraint still protects active capacity.

Runtime entry points are `owned_effective_coach_availability(date,date)` (at most 31 dates), `save_owned_effective_coach_availability(jsonb)` and `preview_owned_effective_coach_availability(jsonb)`. Commands contain `operationId`, `expectedRevision`, and either complete weekly `hours` or 1–31 complete `dates`. Each date has `date`, `mode` (`custom`, `unavailable`, or `weekly` to remove the override) and `hours`; assigned hours retain the venue ID and exact venue update timestamp. Custom dates require at least one valid hour; other modes require an empty hour list. Weekly and date assignments reject overlap across venues. Conflicts return the current authorized revision; UI consumers can reread their bounded policy without discarding local drafts. Saved/preview results contain the new revision, affected pending-request count and retained sessions with original IDs, times and venue labels, without athlete identity or message content. Preview and save accept the same reviewed command; a real save rechecks current state.

Historical request signatures remain callable through reconciled wrappers; their internal renamed implementations are denied to runtime/browser roles. Existing individual-slot/weekly signatures remain compatible with the effective policy, closure, approval, horizon and group guards. No dependency, environment variable or startup command changed. Rollout must apply the complete migration before using this repository/service code; do not partially deploy functions or drop guards to roll back. Use a reviewed forward repair/backup restoration if rollback is required. Shared local and hosted databases were not migrated or reset; independent migration-baseline/seed work and DEV0256 integration remain separate.
