# MovX Club — Scheduling-only MVP specification

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

MovX is a focused scheduling product for martial-arts coaching. Guests discover platform-approved coaches or clearly labelled fictional demo coaches and their real published availability. Email-authenticated clients request one capacity-one private session, and the coach confirms one client before a booking exists. Approved coaches manage their public profile, training locations, location-aware recurring one-hour availability, request queue and booked schedule.

This standalone project intentionally has no blockchain, wallet, token, pass, credit, payment, group-funding, event-marketplace, follow, post or feed behavior. Completed and archived records for those features are historical evidence only and do not define current product behavior. Solana hackathon development belongs to the separate `fitness-booking-social-app` repository.

## 1. Product status and document authority

This document is the single current product contract for the standalone scheduling repository. It defines target behavior and does not claim every target is already implemented.

The repository contains reusable Next.js, Supabase/PostgreSQL, email identity, coach profile/location, list-first discovery, recurring availability, responsive calendar and Vercel foundations. Direct scheduling-only booking and the removal of obsolete runtime were delivered under completed [COR0011](../tickets/archive/organisatory/COR0011-scheduling-only-product.md). The approval-only request target defined here, including separate coach/client interfaces and atomic conversion of one accepted request into the confirmed booking, is delivered under completed [COR0016](../tickets/archive/organisatory/COR0016-coach-confirmed-booking-requests.md).

Tickets record implementation scope and evidence; they do not override this specification. Archived tickets remain immutable history. [DEV0140](../tickets/archive/organisatory/DEV0140-adopt-scheduling-only-contract.md) records the decision to create this focused product split.

### Proposed implementation defaults

The following are implementation defaults rather than additional user decisions:

- Availability is materialized as dated one-hour occurrences for a rolling seven-day window from recurring weekly rules.
- Repeating a request while the same client already has an active attempt for that occurrence returns the existing request rather than creating a duplicate.
- Expiry may be reconciled transactionally during participant/public reads and request mutations for the first release; no background scheduler is required by the product contract.
- A coach or client cancellation records who cancelled and when; P0 does not require a reason field or notification delivery.
- Historical migrations may retain unused wallet/payment/event tables for safe forward compatibility. Current code does not read or expose them.
- Referenced training locations are deactivated rather than destructively deleted; changing the primary location is one atomic replacement.
- A location selected from optional provider search becomes bounded coach-owned profile data. It does not silently create a global gym or point-of-interest catalogue record.
- The location-aware calendar uses stable text cues in addition to color so every assigned venue remains understandable without color perception.

## 3. Actors and authority

### Guest

A guest can browse the public story, coach list, coach profiles, confirmed locations and future open times. A guest cannot submit a request or mutate schedule state.

### Client

A client has a verified email-backed MovX account and application profile. The client can request one eligible future occurrence with another coach, view only their own pending-request status, upcoming confirmed sessions and retained history in `My sessions`, withdraw their own pending request, cancel their own future confirmed booking and optionally manage a private account avatar. A terminal unsuccessful attempt does not consume capacity and may be followed by a new attempt while that occurrence remains eligible. Ordinary clients plus pending and rejected coach applicants receive `My sessions` as their private primary-navigation destination and no `Coach workspace` item. An account avatar does not become public coach media merely because the account later applies to coach.

### Coach

A coach retains client capability, so approved and fictional demo coaches keep `My sessions` and additionally receive `Coach workspace` in desktop and mobile navigation. Their main `Profile` item opens coach Profile by default, while the bottom-left and upper-right account controls continue to open the personal account Profile. Coach Profile, Schedule and Bookings share one Profile-first sub-navigation at a stable top position, followed by the green view-specific hero and then that view's content; Schedule contains location-aware recurring availability and dated capacity status, while Bookings alone contains the private paginated request queue, detailed booked schedule, authorized client identity/avatar and booking actions. After platform approval, the owner can publish their coach profile, manage multiple confirmed training locations, select one active primary location, optionally manage a separate public coach portrait, assign recurring availability to actual locations, accept or decline pending requests, close one unavailable occurrence, inspect confirmed bookings, cancel a future confirmed booking and mark an elapsed confirmed booking completed. A pending or rejected applicant may prepare a private draft but has no public coach or scheduling authority. A suspended coach receives the same two private destinations, with bounded Bookings access needed to resolve existing confirmed sessions, but accepts no pending request, receives no new request and makes no public-media change. A coach cannot see unrelated requests, client bookings or private identity data.

### Platform reviewer

Until the internal admin panel exists, an authorized operator uses a guarded owner-only command to approve, reject or suspend an exact coach application. Every decision records the expected prior state, reviewer/source, bounded reason, verification-policy version and immutable audit event. Ordinary application/runtime roles cannot review applications or grant coach authority.

### MovX service

MovX verifies the Supabase session, establishes transaction-local actor context, enforces row-level authorization and performs bounded database mutations. It does not invent coaches, availability, requests or bookings when configuration/database access is unavailable.

## 4. Primary user flows

### 4.1 Register as a client or apply to coach

1. A visitor requests a one-time email code and verifies it through Supabase Auth.
2. MovX creates or restores the same minimal application profile.
3. The visitor follows the `Find a coach` path and can use client behavior immediately, or follows `Become a coach` and submits a coach application.
4. A pending applicant may prepare a hidden coach-profile draft but cannot publish availability or receive booking requests.
5. A guarded platform review approves, rejects or suspends the application atomically with audit evidence.
6. Approval unlocks coach publication and scheduling without removing client capability, and primary navigation adds `Coach workspace` alongside `My sessions`. Approval does not automatically make the profile visible.
7. The account may manage a private avatar from Account Profile. An approved coach separately chooses whether to add a public portrait to the coach profile.

### 4.2 Discover a coach and time

1. A guest opens Home and can immediately submit the supported coach/focus, discipline and location search or inspect a bounded alphabetical preview read from the authoritative public coach directory. Explanation and coach-application content follow discovery rather than displacing it.
2. Search submits normalized `q`, discipline and location values to Explore. Unsupported date, weekly availability, proximity, price, rating and review controls do not appear.
3. Explore returns the complete server-backed filtered list of visible coaches. Desktop places that list beside the primary-location map; mobile defaults to list and uses canonical `view=list|map` state without losing filters or browser navigation.
4. OpenFreeMap-backed discovery shows each visible coach once at that coach's confirmed active primary location without an application map key. Map failure returns or retains the complete list and profile links.
5. The primary marker describes the coach's public training base, not live presence or today's venue. Explore has no date selector and keeps the coach visible when there is no open time today; any today summary labels the actual occurrence locations or reports `No availability today`.
6. A coach profile shows bounded public details, an optional public portrait, every confirmed active training location with the primary one labelled, and future open dated one-hour occurrences grouped or labelled by actual location. One provider-neutral map fits all active locations; distinct matching marker/list colors and numeric labels aid comparison without replacing names and addresses, and map failure leaves the list usable. Missing portraits use initials rather than a fabricated image.
7. No placeholder coach, location or time is shown when persistent directory or availability data is empty or unavailable.

### 4.3 Request a private session

1. A signed-in client selects one open future occurrence belonging to another visible coach. No fixed minimum-notice interval applies.
2. One authorized database operation rechecks actor/dataset/coach/time/status boundaries and creates or recovers that client's active pending request. It sets the decision deadline to the earlier of 12 hours after the database submission time or occurrence start, and leaves the occurrence open.
3. The client sees `Request saved`, `Awaiting coach confirmation`, `This session is not confirmed yet` and the concrete decision deadline in `Europe/Berlin`. The response contains no other candidate identity, count or status.
4. Other eligible clients may independently request the same occurrence. No pending request holds capacity, and coach-facing result sets remain bounded through pagination even though no candidate cap applies.
5. Reload derives the same active request from PostgreSQL. The client can withdraw it; after withdrawal, expiry, coach decline or non-selection, the client may create a new attempt only if the occurrence is still open and future.

### 4.4 Decide booking requests

1. The owning coach opens the private paginated request queue in Bookings and reviews requests grouped by occurrence.
2. The coach may decline one pending request, accept one pending request or close that one occurrence when no longer available.
3. Acceptance locks the occurrence and active requests together, creates one confirmed capacity-one booking for the selected client, marks the occurrence booked, accepts that request and closes every other pending request as not selected in one transaction. Those clients see neutral `Not confirmed` or `Request closed` presentation rather than competition-oriented wording.
4. Decline terminalizes only that attempt. It leaves the occurrence and other pending requests open, and the same client may submit another eligible attempt.
5. Closing the occurrence marks it withdrawn and closes all pending requests as unavailable in one transaction. It does not edit the recurring availability rule or create a booking.
6. A request that reaches its decision deadline expires without consuming capacity. That deadline is normally 12 hours after submission but is capped at occurrence start for a late request. Concurrent accept/decline/withdraw/expire/close operations resolve to one authoritative idempotent outcome.

### 4.5 Cancel a booking

1. The owning client or coach selects a future confirmed booking.
2. One authorized operation records the cancellation actor/time and reopens the future occurrence.
3. Repeating the same cancellation returns the same terminal outcome; a different or unauthorized actor cannot mutate it. Reopening the occurrence never revives earlier request attempts, so clients must submit new requests while it remains eligible.

### 4.6 Complete a session

1. After the scheduled start, the owning coach can mark a confirmed booking completed.
2. Completion is terminal and idempotent. Clients and unrelated coaches cannot complete it.
3. P0 does not claim independent attendance proof or resolve service disputes.

### 4.7 Manage locations and publish availability

1. An approved coach adds or confirms one or more provider-neutral training locations and selects exactly one active location as primary before publishing the profile.
2. The coach chooses an active location in Schedule, assigns one or more weekly cells to it locally and may switch locations to continue composing the complete working-week draft.
3. One explicit authorized save validates ownership, active-location state, the expected baseline and per-coach time overlap before replacing the complete recurring rule set and synchronizing dated occurrences once.
4. Every dated occurrence copies the actual assigned location snapshot. Changing the primary location never moves an existing occurrence or booking.
5. Deactivating a location stops new availability there, withdraws eligible future open occurrences and closes their pending requests while preserving booked and terminal history.

## 5. Scheduling state model

### Application profile

One profile belongs to one verified Supabase Auth identity inside a dataset. It stores the bounded display identity used for authorization and presentation.

### Coach application and review

One current coach application belongs to one application profile. Absence means not applied; submitted applications move through `pending`, `approved`, `rejected` or `suspended`. Review events are append-only evidence of platform decisions. Historical self-service activation may remain stored for continuity but grants no current coach authority.

### Coach profile and training locations

One owner-scoped coach profile contains public name, slug, biography, disciplines, visibility, one scheduling timezone and an optional public portrait reference. It owns one or more confirmed provider-neutral training-location records; each record has stable identity, kind, public label, coordinates, provenance and active/inactive lifecycle, and may optionally reference a reviewed gym. Exactly one active location is primary while the profile is visible. That primary record supplies the coach's single Explore marker and public training-base label, but it does not establish live presence, choose an occurrence venue or grant gym/account authority.

Pending/rejected applicants may keep only a private draft; a user-created profile enters public discovery only while its application is approved, its owner selects visible and one confirmed active primary location exists. Provider-search selection stores only the bounded coach-owned location the coach explicitly confirms and does not populate a global point-of-interest catalogue. Referenced locations are deactivated rather than destructively deleted so scheduling history remains resolvable.

### Profile images

Image bytes live in Supabase Storage rather than PostgreSQL or the Vercel deployment filesystem. PostgreSQL stores only bounded source/path/version metadata. Private account avatars and public coach portraits use separate storage/access contracts; attaching an image never grants account or coach authority. Signed-in account presentation—including the desktop bottom-left account control, upper-right header control and Profile—prefers its private avatar and otherwise may display the owner's already-public coach portrait as a read-only fallback. A regular account therefore shows its private avatar or initials, while a coach can additionally fall back to the public portrait. Client `My sessions` cards may project the booked coach's current public portrait and link its complete visible identity to the existing public coach profile; a missing, removed or unreadable portrait retains initials. The owning coach may retrieve a request participant's current private avatar through a request-scoped authenticated application route for as long as that request record is retained, including terminal recent-request history. The owning coach of a confirmed booking may separately retrieve that booking client's current private avatar through a booking-scoped route while the booking remains confirmed. Public callers, clients and unrelated coaches receive no image bytes, and the browser never receives the private Storage object path. These fallbacks and bounded reads do not copy media between buckets or change which endpoint removes each image.

JPEG, PNG and WebP source selections are accepted for the first release. The browser corrects orientation, square-crops and downsizes before upload; the server treats that output as untrusted, validates and re-encodes one canonical 512 × 512 WebP at quality 82, strips metadata and retains no original. The selected source is limited to 8 MiB and the canonical upload to 512 KiB. Replacement writes a new immutable object path before retiring the prior owned object so content-delivery-network caches do not retain an overwritten image. Removal restores the initials fallback.

### Recurring availability and dated occurrence

A recurring weekly rule belongs to exactly one active training location owned by the same coach and creates deterministic dated one-hour occurrences in the coach's single scheduling timezone. One weekday/time cell belongs to at most one location. An occurrence is `open`, `booked` or `withdrawn`, carries the immutable snapshot of the rule's actual location and belongs to exactly one coach. An open occurrence may have multiple independent pending requests without being held or booked. Overlapping rules/occurrences are rejected across all of the coach's locations. Changing which location is primary does not change a rule or occurrence.

### Booking request

A booking request is one client's attempt to receive coach confirmation for one occurrence. The same client has at most one active request for that occurrence, while terminal attempts remain immutable history and do not prevent a later eligible attempt. There is no per-occurrence candidate cap; private coach queries use bounded pagination. Its lifecycle is:

```text
Pending -> Accepted
Pending -> Declined
Pending -> Withdrawn
Pending -> Expired
Pending -> Not selected
Pending -> Occurrence closed
```

`Accepted` creates and links exactly one confirmed booking. Accepting one request makes every other pending request for that occurrence internally `Not selected`; closing or otherwise withdrawing the occurrence makes them `Occurrence closed`. Client presentation uses neutral `Not confirmed`, `Request closed` or `Session unavailable` language and never describes competition. All other terminal outcomes consume no capacity. Minimum request fields are request/attempt ID, occurrence/coach/client IDs, scheduled time and actual location snapshot, status, creation/update timestamps, decision deadline capped at occurrence start, decision actor/time and an optional accepted-booking ID. The client projection contains only that client's attempt and never exposes other request identifiers, identity, count, status or content.

### Private booking

A private booking is created only when the owning coach accepts a pending request and binds that occurrence, coach profile and selected client profile. Its lifecycle is:

```text
Confirmed -> Cancelled
Confirmed -> Completed
```

Minimum projection fields are booking ID, occurrence ID, coach/client IDs, scheduled start/end, actual occurrence-location snapshot, status, creation/update timestamps, cancellation actor/time and completion time. It contains no wallet, pass, credit, token, price, transaction signature or chain reference.

## 6. Application and infrastructure boundaries

- Browser code receives only public configuration and bounded projections.
- Next.js server code verifies sessions and owns authorization/database calls.
- Supabase Auth establishes email identity; editable profile fields do not establish authority.
- PostgreSQL is authoritative for profiles, coach applications/reviews, locations, availability, occurrences, booking requests and confirmed bookings.
- Supabase Storage owns normalized image objects. Private account-avatar access and public coach-portrait delivery remain separate; PostgreSQL stores only their bounded references.
- The location module isolates MapLibre/OpenFreeMap rendering and optional Geoapify gym/place search; stored provider-neutral coach-location and occurrence snapshots remain application authority, and legacy provider snapshots remain readable without calling their former provider. The module does not decide primary status, schedule assignment or coach authority.
- Vercel runs the native Next.js application. Hosted secrets are encrypted server-only environment values.
- Historical blockchain/social tables may remain in migration history but are not current runtime dependencies.

## 7. Requests, booking and recovery

Request creation, withdrawal, decline, expiry, acceptance, occurrence closure, booking cancellation and completion are atomic database operations with stable UUID identity and explicit actor context. Start/deadline checks use database instants, not the browser clock. The active-booking uniqueness rule prevents double confirmation even when several requests exist or coach actions race.

A request is eligible only when the occurrence is open, belongs to another visible coach and starts after the database statement time. No fixed minimum notice applies because only coach acceptance assigns capacity. Creation fixes its deadline at `least(created_at + 12 hours, scheduled_start_at)`, so a late request never remains pending after training begins. The Berlin-only interface renders these instants in `Europe/Berlin`, including daylight-saving changes, without changing stored authority.

A repeated same-client submission returns that client's active request. A terminal unsuccessful attempt—withdrawn, expired, declined, not selected or closed with the occurrence—remains history but permits a new attempt while the occurrence is still open and eligible. No candidate cap applies. Coach request queries are paginated, and client projections never contain another request or aggregate candidate count.

Acceptance locks the occurrence and relevant requests in deterministic order, creates one confirmed booking, marks the selected request accepted and occurrence booked, and closes every other pending request as not selected. Coach occurrence closure uses the same concurrency boundary to withdraw that occurrence and close its pending requests without selecting anyone. Decline closes one attempt; withdrawal belongs only to its requesting client; bounded reconciliation expires overdue requests. Every mutation returns the state that actually won rather than false success.

Cancellation locks the confirmed booking/occurrence together, records the authorized actor and reopens only a future occurrence. It does not revive earlier request attempts; interested clients submit new requests if the reopened occurrence is still open and future. Completion locks the booking and requires the owning coach plus an elapsed scheduled start. Terminal request and booking transitions are idempotent and cannot be reversed.

## 8. Identity, data and demo integrity

- Public fictional coaches and venues are demonstration data and are not real endorsements or verified professionals.
- Fictional fixtures display `Demo coach`, never the real platform-verification badge. Existing self-activation or profile presence does not silently become approval.
- `Verified coach` means MovX reviewed the account holder's identity and application under the recorded `movx-identity-application-v1` policy. It is not a licensing, background-check, competence or safety guarantee.
- A `demo run` is an isolated demonstration dataset, not a fitness activity or login session.
- Public pages never expose Auth identifiers, email addresses, database credentials or private client records.
- Private account avatars are not returned by public coach discovery or client-side request projections. The owning coach receives bounded request-client identity with a request-scoped opaque avatar URL in pending and terminal queue rows and a separate booking-scoped URL for a confirmed booking, while Storage paths remain server-only; a coach portrait is public only through the explicit coach-profile media path.
- Only a request's client and owning coach may read its private projection, and the client receives no other candidate identity, count, status or content. Only the confirmed booking's client and coach may read that booking projection; coach discovery exposes no client schedule.
- Empty and unavailable states remain explicit. Fixture-only bookings or times must not masquerade as persisted runtime state.
- Daniel Park is the only fictional coach seeded with a generated portrait in the first image slice. Other demo coaches intentionally prove the initials fallback; generated faces do not represent real professionals.
- `hoang@users.movx.test` is a persistent local passwordless test account for manual account-avatar validation. It is not a hosted seed, production identity or coach account.
- No screen claims payment, wallet verification, blockchain finality or financial protection.

## 9. Scheduling behavior and permissions

- Public marketplace routes use one compact top header at desktop and mobile widths. Private routes keep actor-aware workspace navigation, including a persistent signed-in mobile bottom bar that remains visible while the active page scrolls; coach-only links never appear for an unauthorized actor.
- Authenticated routes use one shared modern workspace hierarchy across desktop sidebar/topbar, mobile bottom navigation, page headings, status summaries, cards, forms and empty/unavailable states. `My sessions` distinguishes the account's pending requests, upcoming confirmed sessions and retained history; coach Profile, Schedule and Bookings retain their stable order and existing responsibilities.
- Home places the working `q`/discipline/location search and a bounded alphabetical server-backed coach preview before explanatory content. Public failure/empty state never substitutes hard-coded coaches, locations, availability, proximity or recommendation claims.
- Desktop Explore keeps the complete filtered list beside the primary-location map. Mobile defaults to list and accepts only canonical `view=list|map` presentation state while retaining supported search values, browser history and automatic list recovery when the map is unavailable.
- Public coach discovery includes visible approved user profiles and explicitly labelled visible fictional fixtures, each with exactly one confirmed active primary marker and all active locations available on its public profile.
- Explore keeps every otherwise visible coach on the map at the primary location regardless of today's availability. It has no date selector in this delivery; today's actual session venues or `No availability today` are presentation details and never move or remove the primary marker.
- Each public coach profile maps all of that coach's confirmed active training locations, selects the primary location initially and repeats each distinct marker color and number in the accessible location list. Inactive history is omitted, text identifies every place without relying on color, and map failure does not remove or disable the list or schedule.
- A pending or rejected applicant may edit only a private coach-profile draft. Only an approved coach owner publishes that profile, manages public training locations or edits recurring availability.
- A verified account owner may replace/remove only their private account avatar. Only an approved coach owner may replace/remove their separately public coach portrait; image ownership never substitutes for coach approval.
- A visible coach has exactly one confirmed active primary location. Replacing primary is atomic, and the current primary cannot be deactivated without a valid replacement.
- A coach composes recurring availability as a local location-aware working-week draft: the selected active location is assigned to empty cells, every selected cell exposes its location through text as well as any color cue, and one explicit authorized save replaces the complete rule set and synchronizes dated occurrences once.
- Deactivating a location removes its active recurring rules, withdraws eligible future open occurrences and closes their pending requests without moving or deleting booked/terminal snapshots.
- A suspended coach is removed from discovery, cannot publish availability, receives no new requests and cannot accept an existing pending request; pending requests close as unavailable while existing confirmed-booking history and bounded terminal actions remain available to the owning parties.
- Only a verified signed-in client submits or withdraws their own booking request, and never against their own coach profile.
- Every occurrence uses coach approval. A client request leaves it open; no instant-booking or coach booking-mode setting exists.
- Request creation remains available until training starts. Each request expires at the earlier of 12 hours after submission or occurrence start. Eligibility uses database instants and the first-release interface displays deadlines in `Europe/Berlin`.
- Only the occurrence's owning coach accepts or declines its pending requests. Acceptance confirms exactly one booking and atomically closes the rest; decline leaves the occurrence and other requests open.
- The owning coach may close one open occurrence without selecting a request. Closure withdraws only that occurrence, closes its pending requests and does not edit its recurring rule.
- A client may submit a new attempt after an unsuccessful terminal outcome while the occurrence remains open and future.
- Only the booking client or coach cancels a future confirmed booking.
- Only the booking coach completes an elapsed confirmed booking.
- Unrelated accounts receive no booking identity or mutation capability.
- Removing a recurring rule does not silently delete terminal booking history.

## 10. Security and failure invariants

- Actor context is derived from a verified session and set transaction-locally.
- Direct table permissions remain least privilege; security-definer functions validate actor context and exact identifiers.
- Coach approval/rejection/suspension is unavailable to ordinary runtime roles. Decisions are expected-state guarded and atomically append immutable review evidence.
- Editable profile fields, Auth metadata, URL parameters and historical activation timestamps never establish approval or badge state.
- Capacity-one enforcement exists in PostgreSQL, not only in browser button state.
- One active request per client/occurrence and one confirmed booking per occurrence are enforced in PostgreSQL, not only in interface state.
- Acceptance and occurrence closure lock the occurrence and relevant requests deterministically so concurrent decisions cannot produce two winners or partial closure.
- Cross-run/cross-coach location identifiers, hidden coaches, self-requesting, elapsed/non-open occurrences and invalid lifecycle transitions fail closed.
- PostgreSQL enforces one active primary location for a visible coach and prevents overlapping active occurrences across all of that coach's locations; browser colors, selectors and marker state are not authority.
- Mutation errors return bounded messages without database/Auth internals.
- Browser inputs are normalized and bounded; server/domain validation is authoritative.
- Uploaded images reject unsupported types and oversized/malformed data, are decoded and re-encoded server-side with metadata removed, and use immutable owner-scoped object paths. Browser-reported names, types and dimensions are not trusted.
- Private request/booking queries are actor-scoped and reveal only necessary identity/display/time/location/status fields. Client responses contain no other-candidate record or aggregate count; unbounded candidate lists are never returned in one response.
- Hosted builds reject loopback/incomplete database/Auth configuration without printing values.

## 11. Definition of done

The scheduling-only MVP is complete when:

- public Home leads with a working normalized coach/focus, discipline and location search plus a bounded alphabetical preview from the authoritative directory, while honest empty/unavailable states and pending-confirmation language remain intact;
- public routes use a compact top-navigation shell without a left sidebar or mobile bottom bar, while signed-in private routes retain capability-aware navigation and a persistent unobstructed mobile bottom bar;
- authenticated `My sessions`, Coach workspace and Profile surfaces use one coherent responsive visual system without changing their routes, actor permissions, privacy boundaries or authoritative actions;
- desktop Explore presents the complete filtered list beside its bounded sticky primary-location map, and mobile Explore can switch canonical list/map state without losing filters, history, profile access or map-failure recovery;
- guests can browse a truthful list of fictional coaches and future open times, with every visible coach pinned once at the confirmed primary training location, truthful today/no-availability presentation and a usable list fallback when the map fails;
- one verified email account can enter through the client or coach-application path while retaining the same underlying identity and client capability;
- a pending applicant cannot self-approve, publish, manage availability or receive booking requests;
- a guarded platform review can approve/reject/suspend an application with immutable evidence, and only current real approval produces the bounded verified badge;
- an approved coach can publish/edit a visible profile with multiple confirmed training locations, exactly one active primary location and recurring one-hour availability assigned to actual locations; visitors can compare all active locations on a color/number-keyed map with a complete list fallback;
- a coach can compose multiple location assignments locally and save the complete working week once, while cross-location overlap or stale/inactive location input fails without partial replacement;
- every authorized account receives `My sessions` in primary navigation while approved, demo and suspended coaches additionally receive `Coach workspace`; approved/demo coach Profile defaults to coach Profile while account-avatar controls retain personal Profile access;
- an account may optionally upload/remove a private normalized avatar, and an approved coach may separately upload/remove a public normalized portrait; all missing/failed image states retain initials;
- a client's `My sessions` cards show the booked coach's current public portrait or initials and link the complete identity to the public coach profile;
- an owning coach can recognize request participants in pending and terminal request rows and the client on a confirmed booking by the current private avatar or stable initials, without making that image public or visible to unrelated actors;
- a different signed-in client can submit one request for an eligible occurrence, reload the same pending attempt and see `Awaiting coach confirmation` plus its Berlin decision deadline without confirmed/reserved language;
- multiple clients can request one occurrence without holding it or learning any other candidate identity, count or status; coach and client result sets stay bounded despite no candidate cap;
- the owning coach can accept exactly one request, producing one confirmed booking and atomically closing every other pending request as not selected, while concurrent accept/close decisions cannot create partial or double confirmation;
- a coach can decline one attempt while leaving the occurrence open, and the client can submit a new request after decline, withdrawal or expiry while it remains future;
- the owning coach can close one unavailable occurrence, withdrawing it and closing all pending requests without editing the recurring availability rule;
- request creation remains available until training starts, each decision deadline is the earlier of 12 hours after submission or occurrence start, and database instants display correctly in `Europe/Berlin`;
- primary-location or training-location changes cannot move the actual location snapshot of an existing occurrence or booking;
- the booking client or coach can cancel a future confirmed booking and the occurrence becomes open again;
- cancellation never revives earlier request attempts; an eligible reopened occurrence requires new requests;
- the owning coach can complete an elapsed confirmed booking;
- unauthorized/self/cross-run/invalid-state mutations fail without partial state;
- removed wallet, blockchain, pass/payment, group-event and social/feed routes are unavailable;
- relevant unit, database, authorization, static, build and responsive browser checks pass;
- a hosted Vercel/Supabase rehearsal completes discovery, two-client request, one coach acceptance, automatic non-selection, cancellation/new-request recovery and coach availability/complete flows without manual database repair.

## 12. Delivery milestones

1. **M0 — Scheduling contract:** adopt this specification and reconcile obsolete marketplace work.

## 14. Demo

1. A guest opens Explore, sees each visible coach once at the labelled primary training location and opens a fictional coach whose today status names the actual availability venue or explicitly reports none.

6. A coach maintains two training locations, chooses one primary map base, assigns recurring hours to actual venues in one saved draft, closes one unavailable occurrence with pending requests, views a prepared elapsed booking and marks it completed without moving its stored location.

The demonstration proves request/booking authority, candidate privacy and concurrency only. It makes no claim about payment, attendance, professional credentials or dispute resolution.
